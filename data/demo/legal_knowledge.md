# CyberShield Demo Knowledge Base

This local knowledge base is for demonstration only. It is not legal advice and should be replaced by verified legal content before any real deployment.

## Cyber harassment support guidance
Online harassment reports should capture dates, platform names, URLs, usernames, repeated messages, threats, screenshots, and witness details. Victims should avoid direct confrontation if escalation is possible and should preserve evidence before blocking accounts.

## Blackmail and extortion support guidance
Blackmail or extortion reports should include the demand, deadline, payment instructions, account identifiers, screenshots, messages, and any threats of disclosure. Urgent escalation is recommended when there are threats of physical harm, sexual image abuse, or immediate financial coercion.

## Hacking and unauthorized access support guidance
Unauthorized access reports should record affected accounts, time of discovery, suspicious logins, password reset messages, financial impact, devices used, and any linked recovery email or phone changes. Victims should change passwords, enable multi-factor authentication, and preserve login alerts.

## Impersonation support guidance
Impersonation reports should include the fake profile URL, screenshots, platform, copied photos, harmful posts, and any messages sent to others. The victim should preserve profile evidence before reporting the fake account to the platform.

## Doxxing and privacy exposure support guidance
Doxxing reports should include exposed personal information, where it appeared, screenshots, URLs, date/time, threats, and evidence of distribution. The victim may need immediate safety planning if home address, workplace, or family information is exposed.

## Financial fraud support guidance
Financial fraud reports should include transaction references, payment channel, amount, time, account identifiers, seller or suspect profile, chat logs, and bank or wallet notifications. Victims should contact the payment provider quickly and preserve receipts.

## Evidence preservation checklist
Useful evidence includes screenshots, screen recordings, URLs, timestamps, usernames, phone numbers, transaction IDs, email headers, login alerts, and device logs. Evidence should be copied to secure storage and hashed to demonstrate integrity.

## Human review requirement
Any complaint, FIR-style draft, legal notice draft, or escalation decision must be reviewed by a qualified human professional or authorized support worker before submission.
