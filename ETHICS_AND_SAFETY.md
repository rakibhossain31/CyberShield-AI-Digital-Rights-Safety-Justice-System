# Ethics and Safety Note

CyberShield AI is designed for a sensitive domain. A system like this can help victims only if it is privacy-preserving, transparent, and human-centered.

## Core principles

1. Victim safety first.
2. No automated legal conclusions.
3. No public offender exposure.
4. Human-in-the-loop review for every legal output.
5. Strong privacy protection and minimal data collection.
6. Auditability for every action.
7. Bias testing before any real-world use.

## Data policy

Use synthetic data for demonstrations and GitHub. Do not upload real complaints, chats, screenshots, phone numbers, addresses, or identities.

## Responsible deployment checklist

- Security review
- Legal review
- Privacy impact assessment
- Role-based access control
- Encryption at rest and in transit
- User consent and data retention rules
- Crisis support pathway
- Human review workflow
- Bias and error testing
- Incident response plan
